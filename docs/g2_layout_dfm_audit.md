## Executive Summary

The **Fused Glass Lighting Rev G2** physical PCB layout ([`fgl_g2_F_Cu_top_copper.png`](../02_pcb_layers/fgl_g2_F_Cu_top_copper.png), [`fgl_g2_B_Cu_bottom_copper_viewed_from_top.png`](../02_pcb_layers/fgl_g2_B_Cu_bottom_copper_viewed_from_top.png)) is manufacturable on JLCPCB's standard 2-layer process and confirms the electrical fixes from the October 24 netlist audit ([`g2_netlist_audit.md`](g2_netlist_audit.md)) and October 25 D16 update ([`g2_decisions.md`](g2_decisions.md)). ERC and DRC pass with zero electrical or clearance errors ([`fgl_g2_DRC.rpt`](fgl_g2_DRC.rpt), [`fgl_g2_ERC.rpt`](fgl_g2_ERC.rpt)).

However, visual inspection of the copper pours, trace routing geometry, silkscreen layers, and 3D assembly models reveals several **physical layout and DFM observations** that script-based netlist verification did not flag:

1. **3D Mechanical / EMI Overlap (`J3` Radar Module over `L2`):** When plugged directly into socket `J3`, the top-left corner of the `HLK-LD2410S` radar daughterboard physically overhangs the bottom edge of SEPIC inductor `L2` (`SWPA8040S100MT`) and sits immediately adjacent to the `R12`/`Q1` keep-alive load.
2. **Overlapping Diagonal Slot Cuts in Ground Pours (`F.Cu` & `B.Cu`):** Long diagonal signal/sense traces (`VOUT`, `DIM_PWM`, `ISEL` on `F.Cu`; `RS2_B`, `ISEL`, `FB` on `B.Cu`) cross over one another in the region between the XIAO (`J10`) and the SEPIC (`U2`), creating overlapping slot cuts in both top and bottom ground pours.
3. **Solid Pour Connections (Zero Thermal Spokes) on `J3` Pin 2 and `J4` Pin 2:** Both radar connector `GND` through-hole pins merge directly into the solid `F.Cu` and `B.Cu` ground pours without thermal relief spokes. While acceptable for automated wave soldering, manual rework or desoldering of `J3` or `J4` will require significant preheating.
4. **Close Via-to-Pad Necking & Tight Rework Clearances:** Several untented/narrow-necked GND vias sit immediately adjacent to 0603/0402 pads (`C3`, `C7`, `C8`, `C16`, `R18`), and the newly added 0402 soft-start capacitor `C16` (`(158.6, 121.1)`) is tightly sandwiched between `Q2` (`(158.6, 118.6)`) and `R13` (`(158.6, 122.4)`).
5. **Missing Silkscreen Designators & Crowded Text:** Six passive components (`R13`, `R15`, `R16`, `C2`, `C4`, `C16`) have no reference designators on `F.Silkscreen`, and the labels under the XIAO (`C15R25R24 R6`) run together with near-zero spacing.

## 1. Component Placement & Partitioning

### 1.1 Local IC Decoupling
* **`U2` (TPS61500) Supply Bypassing:** `C3` (100 nF 0603) is placed at `(141.9, 118.6)`, ~2.8 mm directly above `U2` pin 3 (`VIN`) on `F.Cu` ([`fgl_g2_F_Cu_top_copper.png`](../02_pcb_layers/fgl_g2_F_Cu_top_copper.png)). Input bulk capacitors `C1` and `C2` (2 × 10 µF 0805) sit upstream along the `+5V` spine near `Q2` and `L1` at `(155.6, 118.4)` and `(153.6, 118.4)`. Because SEPIC input current is continuous through `L1` rather than pulsed at the `VIN` pin, placing `C3` at pin 3 and `C1`/`C2` on the `+5V` feed is electrically sound. Sliding `C2` 4–5 mm left toward `L1` pad 1 (`(148.0, 121.0)`) in a future spin would further shorten the high-frequency input ripple loop through `L1`.
* **SEPIC Output Decoupling (`C5`, `C6`):** Resized in D16 to a 1210 package (10 µF 100 V X7S) alongside `C6` (100 nF 100 V 0603), `C5` (`(136.5, 133.0)`) and `C6` (`(136.5, 135.6)`) sit immediately next to `D5`'s cathode (`(142.5, 133.0)`), with their ground pads tied into the top and bottom ground planes right below `U2`'s exposed thermal pad.
* **MCU & Radar Rail Bypassing (`C10`/`C11`, `C13`/`C14`):** `C10` (10 µF) and `C11` (100 nF) are placed immediately above `J10`'s `+3V3` and `GND` socket pins (`(114.9, 134.6)` and `(118.4, 134.6)`). `C13` (10 µF) and `C14` (100 nF) sit right next to ferrite bead `FB1` at `(159.8, 144.6)` and `(159.8, 146.4)` above the `J3` radar socket.

### 1.2 High-Power / Switching Components vs. Sensitive Analog Lines
* **SEPIC Switching Loop Area:** The pulsed high-$dI/dt$ loop (`U2` `SW` → `C4` → `D5` → `C5`/`C6` → `GND` return → `U2` `PGND`/EP) spans roughly 8 × 12 mm in the center of the board ([`fgl_g2_F_Cu_top_copper.png`](../02_pcb_layers/fgl_g2_F_Cu_top_copper.png)). While wider than a minimum-area layout because `D5` sits below `C4` and to the left of `L2`, the unbroken `B.Cu` ground plane directly underneath confines the loop inductance ([`fgl_g2_B_Cu_bottom_copper_viewed_from_top.png`](../02_pcb_layers/fgl_g2_B_Cu_bottom_copper_viewed_from_top.png)).
* **Proximity of `R15`/`R16` (`OVP`), `R19` (`FREQ`), and `C9` (`COMP`) to Switching Components:** On `F.Cu` and `F.Silkscreen`, `R15` (`(143.0, 129.5)`) and `R16` (`(141.2, 129.5)`) sit tucked between `U2`'s lower pins, `C4` (`(145.0, 128.6)`—carrying the full AC switching swing between `SW` and `SEP_B`), and `D5` (`(142.5, 133.0)`). While `OVP` is only an open-string comparator input, monitor `COMP` (`C9` at `(136.4, 129.5)`) and `FREQ` (`R19` at `(139.4, 129.5)`) for switching noise pickup during bench test `G2-T3`.
* **3D Overhang of `HLK-LD2410S` Radar Module (`J3`) over `L2`:** The 3D renders ([`fgl_g2_3d_top.png`](../03_3d_renders/fgl_g2_3d_top.png), [`fgl_g2_3d_iso.png`](../03_3d_renders/fgl_g2_3d_iso.png)) show that when the 20 × 20 mm radar module is plugged into socket `J3`, its top-left corner physically overlaps the lower body of SEPIC inductor `L2` (`(150.8, 131.2)`) and sits right next to the `R12`/`Q1` keep-alive pulse load (`(161.0, 128.6)`). Although the 24 GHz patch antenna faces upward away from the PCB, the 1.17 MHz switching fringe field from `L2` is only a few millimeters beneath the radar's baseband/LDO circuitry.
* **ADC Analog Isolation (`VSENSE` & `ADC_POT`):** The high-impedance `VSENSE` divider (`R24`/`R25`/`C15`) and potentiometer filter (`R5`/`R6`/`C12`) are cleanly isolated in the bottom-left corner beneath the socketed XIAO module, ~25 mm away from the SEPIC switching node.

## 2. Routing & Signal Integrity

* **Trace Angles & Acid Traps:** All signal and power traces use 45-degree mitered bends or smooth chamfered entries ([`fgl_g2_F_Cu_top_copper.png`](../02_pcb_layers/fgl_g2_F_Cu_top_copper.png), [`fgl_g2_B_Cu_bottom_copper_viewed_from_top.png`](../02_pcb_layers/fgl_g2_B_Cu_bottom_copper_viewed_from_top.png)). The only 90-degree T-junctions are wide power bus taps on the 1.0 mm `LED_RTN` bus (joining `J5`–`J8` pin 3) and the `+5V` spine, which pose no manufacturing or signal-integrity risk.
* **Differential Pairs:** None present. `J1`'s USB `D+`/`D-` pins are unconnected (`NC`), and the XIAO's native USB-C port is self-contained on the plug-in module.
* **Ground Pour Continuity & Overlapping Return-Path Cuts:**
  * Directly beneath the SEPIC converter (`U2`, `L1`, `C4`, `L2`, `D5`, `C5`, `C6`), `B.Cu` maintains an unbroken ground pour stitched by `U2`'s 15 thermal vias and surrounding ground vias ([`fgl_g2_B_Cu_bottom_copper_viewed_from_top.png`](../02_pcb_layers/fgl_g2_B_Cu_bottom_copper_viewed_from_top.png)).
  * In the zone between `J10` (`x ≈ 121`) and `U2` (`x ≈ 136`), two long diagonal traces on `F.Cu` (`DIM_PWM` at 19.53 kHz and `ISEL`) run from bottom-left to top-right, crossing over the long diagonal `VOUT` trace running from `C5`/`TP6` down-left to `R24`, while slicing a long diagonal slot across the `F.Cu` ground pour. At the same time, `B.Cu` in that region is cut by three diagonal tracks (`RS2_B` from `R21` to `JP1.1`, `ISEL` from `JP1.3` to the mid-board via, and the `FB` sense trace from `R21` to `U2.9`). Where these top and bottom slots intersect, high-frequency return currents between the MCU and driver must detour around the slot perimeter. Adding 1–2 GND stitching vias bridging across the `B.Cu` diagonal tracks or routing `VOUT` orthogonally along the perimeter in a future spin would improve ground plane integrity.
* **Kelvin Sense Routing (`FB`):** The 0.25 mm `FB` trace taps `R21`'s `LED_RTN` pad independently of the main current-carrying path and routes on `B.Cu` to `U2` pin 9, preventing LED string IR drop from corrupting the 0.2 V regulation reference.

## 3. DFM & Clearances

* **Silkscreen-to-Pad Clearances & Clipping:**
  * All solder mask openings are cleanly subtracted from `F.Silkscreen` ([`fgl_g2_F_Silkscreen_top_silkscreen.png`](../02_pcb_layers/fgl_g2_F_Silkscreen_top_silkscreen.png)); there is no silkscreen ink overlapping exposed SMD or PTH pads.
  * The two `silk_edge_clearance` warnings in [`fgl_g2_DRC.rpt`](fgl_g2_DRC.rpt) at `(172.8, 112.3)` and `(172.8, 121.7)` are caused by `J1`'s USB-C connector body outline overhanging the right board edge (`x = 174.0`) and are benign.
* **Missing & Crowded Silkscreen Designators:**
  * Six passive components (`R13`, `R15`, `R16`, `C2`, `C4`, `C16`) have their reference designators moved to `F.Fab` and omitted from `F.Silkscreen` ([`fgl_g2_F_Silkscreen_top_silkscreen.png`](../02_pcb_layers/fgl_g2_F_Silkscreen_top_silkscreen.png), [`g2_decisions.md`](g2_decisions.md) D15/D16). While automated SMT placement relies on the CPL coordinates, bench bring-up (such as tuning `R15` or `C9` during `G2-T3`/`G2-T4`) requires referencing the CAD layout view.
  * Under the XIAO module area (`y ≈ 144 mm`), the silkscreen labels `C15`, `R25`, `R24`, and `R6` are concatenated (`C15R25R24 R6`) with almost no horizontal separation.
* **Via-in-Pad & Solder Wicking Assessment:**
  * **Thermal Pad Vias (`U2`):** `U2`'s exposed pad contains a 3 × 5 grid of 15 vias (0.3 mm drill / 0.6 mm diameter), enlarged from 0.2/0.5 mm to meet JLCPCB's standard 2-layer minimum drill rule ([`g2_decisions.md`](g2_decisions.md) D15). Because standard 2-layer vias are not epoxy-filled and capped (VIPPO), minor solder paste wicking into the 0.3 mm barrels during reflow is expected but acceptable with standard window-pane paste apertures.
  * **Adjacent SMD Ground Vias:** No standard SMD component pads contain via-in-pad holes. However, the ground vias next to `C8`, `C7`, `R18`, `C3`, and `C16` sit immediately off the pad edges with very short, wide copper necks ([`fgl_g2_F_Cu_top_copper.png`](../02_pcb_layers/fgl_g2_F_Cu_top_copper.png)). Confirm in the Gerber preview that `F.Mask` tents or bridges between these via annular rings and the adjacent 0402/0603 pads to prevent solder thieves / tombstoning during reflow.
* **Component Courtyard Spacing (`C16` / `Q2` / `R13`):**
  * Added in D16, `C16` (0402 at `(158.6, 121.1)`) sits directly between `Q2` (SOT-23 at `(158.6, 118.6)`) and `R13` (0603 at `(158.6, 122.4)`). The 1.30 mm center-to-center spacing between `C16` and `R13` passes DRC and pick-and-place tolerances, though hand rework with a soldering iron will be tight.
* **Board Edge Clearances:** Both copper pours maintain ≥ 0.5 mm pullback from `Edge.Cuts` and around the four M3 mounting holes (`H1`–`H4`).

## 4. Thermal Management

* **Thermal Reliefs on Ground & Power Pours:**
  * Almost all SMD ground pads (`C1`–`C3`, `C5`–`C15`, `R11`, `R16`, `R18`, `R20`, `R25`, `Q1`, `D1`) and through-hole ground pins (`J1` shield pins, `JP1` pin 2, `J9`/`J10` GND pins) use proper 4-spoke thermal reliefs on both `F.Cu` and `B.Cu` ([`fgl_g2_F_Cu_top_copper.png`](../02_pcb_layers/fgl_g2_F_Cu_top_copper.png), [`fgl_g2_B_Cu_bottom_copper_viewed_from_top.png`](../02_pcb_layers/fgl_g2_B_Cu_bottom_copper_viewed_from_top.png)).
  * **Exception — Solid Pour Connections on `J3` Pin 2 and `J4` Pin 2:** As documented in [`g2_decisions.md`](g2_decisions.md) D15 and visible in the top/bottom copper plots, `J3` pin 2 (radar socket `GND`) and `J4` pin 2 (radar JST-PH `GND`) connect **solidly (without thermal spokes)** to both the `F.Cu` and `B.Cu` ground pours because adjacent signal traces blocked 3 of the 4 standard spoke positions. While JLCPCB's wave-soldering preheat will wet these pins properly during assembly, **hand-soldering or desoldering `J3` or `J4` on the bench will be difficult** without hot-air preheating, as both 1 oz copper planes act as a direct thermal sink on pin 2.
* **Power Dissipation Paths:**
  * `U2` (TPS61500) has a direct 15-via thermal path from its exposed pad (`EP`) into the solid `B.Cu` ground plane to dissipate the ~0.6–0.8 W converter loss at the 5 W LED cap.
  * `D5` (SS36 SMA) has wide copper pours/tracks on both anode (`SEP_B`) and cathode (`VOUT`) pads to spread diode conduction heat (~0.2–0.3 W).
